MATLAB Lunar Lander Simulation

MECE 117 — Intro to Programming for Engineers · Oct–Dec 2024

Built a MATLAB simulation of lunar lander descent with real physics — lunar gravity (1.62 m/s²), variable thrust with fuel consumption, and rotational dynamics — plus a full GUI with arrow-key thrust and rotation controls.

Landing succeeds only if all three conditions are met: touchdown on a designated flat zone, orientation within ±2° of upright, and vertical velocity under 0.5 m/s. Optimized the numerical integration (dt = 0.1s) to keep landing trajectory accuracy within a 5% error margin.
