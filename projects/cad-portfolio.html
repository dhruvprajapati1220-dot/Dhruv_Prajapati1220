SolidWorks CAD Portfolio

Coursework

A collection of mechanical design projects demonstrating SolidWorks CAD modeling, assembly design, and technical documentation across several applications:

  • Car Tire Assembly: detailed tread and sidewall geometry with a full multi-component assembly and technical drawings.
  • Car Battery Design: internal cell arrangement, housing, terminal posts, and exploded assembly views.
  • Mini UGV Prototype: conceptual chassis, suspension layout, and component integration for an autonomous mobile platform.
  • Comprehensive Car Model: full body panels, structural components, and kinematic assembly constraints.

Covers part modeling, multi-part assembly design, orthographic/section technical drawings, GD&T, and design-for-manufacturing principles.
