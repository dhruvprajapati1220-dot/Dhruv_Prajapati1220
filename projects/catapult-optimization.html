3D-Printed Catapult Optimization

Coursework

Designed and manufactured a 3D-printed catapult arm to maximize both launch distance and strength-to-weight ratio under fixed geometric constraints (40mm hole spacing, 175mm max length, single rubber-band power source), validated with a cantilever beam bending test.

Iteration 1 reached a 310" launch distance at a 740 strength-to-weight ratio. Iteration 2 — with FEA-guided material reduction in non-critical regions and reinforcement at high-stress zones — reached 320" (projectile hit the test-room wall) at a 626 ratio, a 3.2% distance gain for a 15% weight increase, and survived a higher bending load.
