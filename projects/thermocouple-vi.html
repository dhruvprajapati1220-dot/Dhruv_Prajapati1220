Thermocouple VI

Engineering Measurements Lab · Coursework

LabVIEW Virtual Instrument for thermocouple-based temperature measurement, real-time monitoring, and data logging — covering the Seebeck effect, thermocouple types (K, J, T), and cold-junction compensation.

Implemented a front panel with live temperature display and trend charting, plus a block diagram handling DAQ configuration, CJC correction, and file logging to CSV/TDMS with configurable alarm thresholds.
